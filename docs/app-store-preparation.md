## Promotional text draft

Build better habits like you are leveling up a champion. Complete daily quests, earn coins and XP, practice battles, and prepare for friendly squad competition.

## Description draft

Questline turns everyday progress into a game.

Choose a campaign, equip a small deck of daily habits, and create your champion. Completing real-life quests awards coins and XP that power your progression. Practice battles make consistency feel rewarding, while the game-inspired dashboard keeps today’s goals clear.

Current version:

- Create a champion and choose a play style
- Turn habits into daily quests
- Earn coins and XP for completed quests
- Track progress privately on your device
- Practice fictional avatar battles
- Schedule an optional daily reminder on iPhone
- Explore demonstration rankings and squad matching

Questline 1.0 works without an account. Online friends, real matchmaking, and public leaderboards are planned future features and are not represented as live services in this version.

## Review notes draft

Questline 1.0 does not require an account. All player profile, habit, coin, and XP data is stored locally. The leaderboard, opponent, and squad surfaces are clearly labeled demonstration content and do not represent real users. Notification permission is requested only after the user taps the daily reminder button. The notification is scheduled locally for 8:00 PM and can be disabled in iOS Settings.
